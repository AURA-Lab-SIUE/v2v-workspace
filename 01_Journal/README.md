# 01_Journal

Your weekly reading reflections.

One file per week. Start from `_templates/journal-entry.md`, and name files so they sort
in order:

```
week-01-what-counts-as-evidence.md
week-02-curiosity-and-trust.md
```

Pick one thinking path (Connector, Troubleshooter, or Critic) and stay in it. A summary
of the reading is not a reflection.

## Length

- **MC 451:** 250 to 300 words, on the assigned chapter.
- **MC 501:** 450 to 500 words, engaging both the chapter and that week's assigned
  reading. An entry that addresses the chapter alone receives a maximum of 7 of 10 points.

## How to turn it in

Two steps, and you need both.

1. **Commit and push** the entry with the rest of your workspace. This is your backup and
   your version history, and it is the habit the course is building.
2. **Submit it in Blackboard**, under that week's assignment. **Grading happens from the
   Blackboard submission.** An entry that was committed but never submitted in Blackboard
   has not been turned in.

## When

- **MC 451:** Friday, 11:59 PM.
- **MC 501:** Monday, 11:59 PM. You are welcome to submit any week early.

## One more thing

If AI shaped any work you submit that week, note in the entry what you used it for and
how you checked its output. This is required by the syllabus in both courses.
