# 03_Project

Your research project, ordered the way the term is ordered.

| Folder | What goes here | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01_Prospectus/` | Research question, design, justification | 6 |
| `02_Codebook/` | The coding instrument | 8 |
| `03_Data/` | Sampled data, coding, analysis | 10 to 12 |
| `04_Drafts/` | Drafts and the final White Paper | 13 to 14 |

You fill these roughly in sequence. By the end this folder holds the whole trail from
your first research question to the finished White Paper, which is what makes the
methods section writable.

Nothing personally identifying about the people in your data. That standard applies to
your own workspace, not just to what you publish.
